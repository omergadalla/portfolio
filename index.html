About Me

Unlike typical coursework-focused students, I have spent the last few years building real systems. From designing a 100N liquid bipropellant engine for the Galaxy Rocket Team to developing green propulsion solutions at MTS Defense, I thrive in multidisciplinary environments requiring analytical depth (CFD, FEA) and practical manufacturing skills.

Technical Projects

Sustainable Bi-propellant Thruster

Tools: NASA CEA, MATLAB, CAD, P&ID

Designed a cost-effective thruster for small satellites (10-100 kg). Validated combustion parameters using NASA CEA and created a MATLAB code to calculate injector, nozzle, and chamber geometry for a 10-bar pressure regime.

High Altitude Rocket (Mach 1.4)

Tools: ANSYS Fluent, Mechanical, Composites

Led aerodynamic/structural design for a 15,000 ft rocket. Performed CFD to minimize drag and FEA to validate the Aluminum 7075 motor block and composite fuselage under high-G flight loads.

Supersonic Nozzle MOC Design

Tools: MATLAB, Method of Characteristics

Developed a custom MATLAB tool to generate an optimized nozzle contour for Mach 3 exit conditions. Implemented a Prandtl-Meyer function solver to ensure smooth supersonic expansion without shocks.

Combustion Analysis (H2/O2)

Tools: MATLAB, Newton-Raphson Method

Created a solver to calculate equilibrium mole fractions of 6 combustion species. Validated reaction kinetics and thermodynamic properties for a Hydrogen-Oxygen rocket engine.
