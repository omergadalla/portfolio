Technical Projects

FLAGSHIP PROJECT · TEKNOFEST B3 · FINALIST

100 N GOX/Jet-A1 Liquid Bipropellant Rocket Engine

From system modelling to the test stand. I led the analysis for the 15-member engine team: every design iteration came through analysis before it was built.

100 N thrust ≈3,000 K combustion 10 s hot-fire design Theory → cold-flow → hot-fire
PHASE 01: SYSTEM MODELLING

Architecture & Sizing.

NASA CEA set the combustion properties; a 1D transient feed-system model in MATLAB/Simscape showed flow response and how the subsystems interact. Together they fixed propellant mass flows and sizing before any CAD was drawn.

> NASA CEA / MATLAB Simscape
PHASE 02: CHAMBER & NOZZLE CFD

Flow Validation.

Combustion CFD of the chamber, then chamber and nozzle flow CFD in ANSYS Fluent to check the performance of a nozzle contour designed by the Method of Characteristics – shock location and nozzle flow properties.

> ANSYS Fluent / Combustion & compressible flow
PHASE 03: THERMAL & STRUCTURAL

Heat-Sink Design.

A 10-second burn at about 3,000 K puts a large thermal load on the walls. I ran a transient thermal analysis of the maximum material temperature over the full burn for a heat-sink cooling strategy with Inconel and copper alloys, then thermo-structural FEA under chamber pressure and thermal load.

> Transient Thermal / Thermo-structural FEA
PHASE 04: ADDITIVE MANUFACTURING

Printed for Precision.

The injector's internal geometry is too complex for conventional CNC, so the combustion chamber and injector were produced by additive manufacturing.

> Propellants: Gaseous Oxygen (GOX) & Jet-A1
PHASE 05: INJECTOR CFD

Coaxial Swirl Injector.

Multiphase CFD of the coaxial swirl injector in two steps: VOF for the internal flow and liquid film, then DPM for the spray – cone angle, droplet breakup and atomisation.

> ANSYS Fluent / VOF + DPM multiphase
PHASE 06: COLD-FLOW TEST

CFD Meets Test.

Before ignition, cold-flow tests made the spray cone visible. I compared the CFD predictions with the cold-flow data and fed the differences back into the next design iteration.

> Cold-flow testing / CFD–test comparison
PHASE 07: TEST STAND

Hardware Integration.

The feed system was assembled on the test stand: solenoid valves, pressure regulation, flow lines and engine mount. I supported valve functional, leak and pressure tests up to 1.5× MEOP and the injector cold-flow tests.

> Feed System / GOX & Jet-A1 / Solenoid Valves
TEKNOFEST ROCKET COMPETITION · A7 · FINALIST

1,200 N N₂O/Jet-A1 Liquid-Propellant Rocket

Galaxy was the only team in the A7 category, building what the team describes as Türkiye's first university-developed original liquid-propellant rocket. I was responsible for vehicle aerodynamics and all engine analyses.

1,200 N thrust 12,000 N·s total impulse 15 bar · O/F 6 PDR & CDR passed Hot-fire tested
01: COMBUSTION CFD

Reacting Flow, Checked Against CEA.

Steady 2D axisymmetric combustion CFD in ANSYS Fluent: k-ω SST, species transport with the Eddy-Dissipation Concept and a 71-species / 1,037-reaction kerosene mechanism. Mean chamber temperature ≈3,000 K; Mach 2.24 at the exit with shock cells in the plume.

Isp, c* and CF were compared with NASA CEA. Both methods agree closely and both sit above the 163 s design-point Isp – mainly because of the adiabatic-wall assumption.

> ANSYS Fluent / EDC / NASA CEA
02: INJECTOR CFD

Triplet Impinging Injector.

Transient three-phase VOF (air, liquid N₂O, liquid Jet-A1) on one O-F-O element. The jets meet at the design impingement point and the liquid sheet of primary atomisation forms. Next step: extend the run to 5–10 ms and couple VOF to DPM for the full spray.

> ANSYS Fluent / VOF / k-ω SST
03: THERMAL ANALYSIS

The Analysis That Set the Cooling Requirement.

Gas-side heat transfer from the Bartz correlation with CEA properties (MATLAB), then a transient thermal analysis of the chamber wall in ANSYS Thermal Desktop. The uncooled wall passes the material limit during the burn, so the analysis turned into a cooling requirement for the engine.

> Bartz / MATLAB / ANSYS Thermal Desktop
04: THERMO-STRUCTURAL FEA

Thermal Loads Into Structure.

The temperature field was mapped into ANSYS Mechanical. Stresses stay below yield everywhere except the region where the chamber integrates with the system, which went back to the design team for a mechanical update.

> ANSYS Mechanical / Thermo-structural

Vehicle Aerodynamics

External aerodynamic CFD in ANSYS Fluent for drag coefficients across the flight envelope, feeding the flight-performance model. System trade studies on total impulse, apogee and propellant mass fraction.

Hot-Fire Testing

Engine hot-fire tests completed. Worked hands-on with the pressure and temperature sensors and analysed the test data.
